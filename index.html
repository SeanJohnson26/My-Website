A Bit About Me

Hey, glad you're here!

I'm Sean — a First Year Coordinator with Awakening Collegiate Fellowship in Milledgeville, GA. I'm a community guy at heart. I love people best by showing up, serving behind the scenes, and being the kind of friend you can count on. Awakening is where I found my best friends, deepened my relationship with Christ, and grew more than I expected. Now I get to be part of building that same community for the next class of students.

ACF is a community of college students who take their faith seriously and love each other well. It's one of my favorite places on earth.
