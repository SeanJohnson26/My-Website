Why Financial Partnership Matters

Campus ministry is uniquely positioned to reach people at a turning-point moment in their lives. College students are asking the biggest questions — about meaning, about God, about how to live — and I get to be right there in the middle of that.

But this work doesn't happen without people who believe in it. Your financial support covers my living expenses, ministry costs, and allows me to invest in students without having to work a second job. Every dollar goes directly toward being present on campus and serving the ACF community.

Whether you give $10 a month or $100 — it matters, and I mean that. I'm genuinely grateful for every single person on this team.
