Dear friends and ministry partners,

Summer is here, and even though campus quiets down a bit, God doesn't take a break — and neither does ACF.

## A Night That Changed Things

A few weeks ago, we gathered for a spontaneous night of prayer and worship. We didn't have a speaker or a special program — just students, a guitar, and a real hunger to meet with God. What happened over the next three hours was one of the most beautiful things I've witnessed in campus ministry.

Students who had never prayed out loud before were standing up, tears running down their faces, saying things like *"I didn't know God could feel this close."* I just stood in the back and watched, honestly in awe. That's the thing about this generation — they're not looking for entertainment. They want the real thing. And when you create the space, they go for it.
