About Me

A little bit about who I am, why I do this, and the ministry I'm a part of.

Hey there!

I'm Sean Johnson

I grew up in Peachtree Corners, GA, and came to Georgia College as a student in August 2022. Since early in college, ministry has played a huge role in shaping both my faith and the direction of my life. I originally got involved with the Wesley Foundation, which later transitioned into Awakening Collegiate Fellowship — and through that community I found something I didn't know I was looking for: authentic discipleship, real mentorship, and a much deeper relationship with Christ.

As I grew spiritually, I started stepping into leadership. I've served on the Community Team for the past 3 years, helping create spaces where students can build relationships and experience Christ-centered community. I served on Fresh, welcoming and mentoring incoming freshmen as they navigated both college and faith. Most recently, I served as a Discipleship Intern — investing in students more intentionally through one-on-one discipleship.

My Calling

What makes ministry especially meaningful to me is that I've personally experienced what it looks like for someone to intentionally pour into your life spiritually. That kind of investment changed me — and because of it, God has grown in me a real desire to do the same for others.

This year, I'll be serving full-time with ACF as a First Year Coordinator, overseeing and helping serve on the Community Team, the Fresh Team, and Alpha. My hope is to help develop leaders, build genuine community, and create spaces where students can encounter Jesus and grow roots that actually last.

The Ministry

About Awakening Collegiate Fellowship

Awakening Collegiate Fellowship of Milledgeville is a Wesleyan-minded campus ministry that partners closely with the Global Methodist Church as well as other Methodist and Wesleyan churches. We minister primarily to students on the Georgia College campus — walking alongside them as they grow in faith, community, and their relationship with Christ.

Our week revolves around three main things. Mondays we host Fresh — our freshmen outreach night featuring worship, a message, and small groups. Tuesdays and Thursdays we run Coffee House from 11–2, where we open up the ACF building, hand out free coffee, and create a space for students to just show up and be. Wednesdays we meet for our main service — a time of fellowship, worship, and a message from our director, Tate. Beyond that, you'll find Bible studies and other gatherings throughout the week, but those three are the heartbeat of what we do.

What I Do

Roles & Responsibilities

Three ministry areas I get to pour into as First Year Coordinator.

As a First Year Coordinator at ACF, my role is less about having all the answers and more about showing up — for interns, for freshmen, for seekers, and for anyone still figuring out where they fit. Alongside raising support and learning the inner workings of ministry firsthand, I'm specifically invested in three areas of the Awakening community.

🌿

Fresh — Freshmen Outreach

Fresh is our Monday night environment built specifically for freshmen — worship, a message, and small groups all led by student leaders. As one of five staff serving here, my focus is relational: checking in with leaders, making genuine connections with freshmen, and hosting Fresh Hang days where real friendships actually get to form.

🎉

Community — Event Planning

The Community team exists to give the Awakening family spaces to just be together. Think formals, Friendsgiving potlucks, Christmas White Elephant, and yes — a Halloween haunted house. I help plan, coordinate, and pull off events that are fun on the surface and genuinely connecting underneath.

🌎

Alpha — Exploring Faith Together

Alpha is a global ministry initiative built around one simple idea: a safe, judgment-free space where anyone can ask honest questions about faith in Christ. New believers, skeptics, the openly curious — all welcome. I help lead and oversee this ministry area at ACF, and it’s honestly one of my favorites to be a part of.

🙏

One-on-One Discipleship

I have the privilege of discipling 3 interns individually, walking alongside them in faith, growth, and calling. Our discipleship is threefold in mission — growing in belief in God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; deepening intimacy with God and with one another; and building accountability and vulnerability within community. Ministry is most personal at this level, and I don’t take it lightly.
